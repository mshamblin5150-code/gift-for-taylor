# Graphite is one Schedule identity with two hand-assigned renderings

The Schedule uses the supplied Clinical Calendar Graphite reference as its
visual family: cool mineral surfaces and silver structure, with emerald kept
to a restrained identity and control signal. Light and dark are both complete,
hand-assigned Material 3 schemes; neither is generated by inverting the other
or by seeding `ColorScheme.fromSeed`. The device follows `System` by default,
as [ADR-0007](0007-theme-belongs-to-the-device-the-mark-does-not.md) decided.
The exact proposed tokens and their calculated contrast pairs live in the
[visual direction research](../research/visual-design-direction.md), not here.

Coverage and roster keep the separate channels settled by
[ADR-0008](0008-a-band-names-a-roster-or-reads-coverage.md). A coverage cell's
fill changes with covered, one short, or several short, and its visible glyph
states the reading at 48 dp. A roster band has no fill: its continuous rule
and stronger Section label carry the boundary. Both schemes assign these
colors separately so the hierarchy remains legible in either brightness.
The 12 sp short reading uses the ordinary-text 4.5:1 contrast gate; its
foreground is checked against the *actual* cell or badge fill.

The printed Schedule book has its own monochrome treatment: white paper,
black text and rules, a neutral Section band, and a lighter weekend tint.
This **replaces the current `#9fc5cc` Section tint** in the proposed print
design. ADR-0008 says that tint remains on paper while the screen changes;
that described the then-current page, not a constraint to preserve the teal
through this separately commissioned print pass. The screen's unfilled roster
rule does not transfer to paper. The print design must be checked at the
one-page fit in grayscale before implementation is accepted.

The app icon uses Clinical Calendar's canonical transparent Axion
delta-and-orbit asset as its mark. The supplied Axion Healthcare image
confirms that identity but includes a wordmark and background unsuitable for
small icons. Ordinary, maskable, favicon, and Apple-touch outputs share the
mark, with a simplified silhouette at favicon sizes and maskable-safe
composition. Browser chrome uses one fixed graphite `#151A1F` theme color in
the manifest and HTML; it does not change with the device theme.

The trade-off is a small set of Schedule-specific tokens and two separately
maintained schemes instead of one generated palette. That cost is intentional:
the source Graphite palette has no light counterpart, and automatic tonal
mapping cannot preserve the coverage ramp, roster boundary, and 48 dp contrast
constraints in both brightnesses. The research values are design targets;
rendered contrast, icon crops, and physical print output remain acceptance
checks for the implementation tickets.
